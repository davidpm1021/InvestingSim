The year we crossed 100,000 teachers.

Where we stood entering 2025

NGPF entered 2025 with curriculum in roughly 82,000 classrooms across all 50 states, deepening adoption in the states that had just passed personal-finance graduation requirements.

Teacher demand for ready-to-teach materials kept climbing — not just for the 9-unit personal finance course, but for math integrations, on-demand PD modules, and our growing FinCamp event series.

This deck walks through what changed over the last twelve months — and where Mission 2030 takes us next.
