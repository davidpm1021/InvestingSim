Guidelines
The goal in creating this Copy Catalog is to provide model language when writing about core NGPF initiatives and products. 

If all team members start with this guidance as a baseline, we’ll: 
standardize the messaging and marketing about our org
reduce (but not fully eliminate) the need for peer editing and proofreading
hopefully save time

How to use this Copy Catalog: 
Find the product or initiative you’re trying to write about
Self-select whether you need the Sentence, the Bullets, or the Paragraph as your baseline text
Modify as needed to suit your needs, including but not limited to: 
Use your judgment on how often and where to include no-cost and/or free
Use your judgment on how often and where to include NGPF in front of initiatives/products
If you’re using the bulleted lists and need more lead-in copy, pull from the Sentence or Paragraph language
Use in conjunction with the NGPF Copywriting Style Guide


See something you think should be changed? Leave a comment next to the item and tag Jessica and Hannah

Mission

Our mission is to revolutionize the teaching of personal finance in all schools and to improve the financial lives of the next generation of Americans.


Boilerplate - About NGPF

Next Gen Personal Finance (NGPF) is a nonprofit committed to guaranteeing that all U.S. high school students receive a personal finance course prior to graduating. NGPF has become the number one source for over 115,000 educators looking for high-quality, engaging personal finance curriculum to equip students with the skills they need to thrive in the future. The organization invests in teacher professional development with live Virtual Professional Development, 11 Certification Courses, and 40+ asynchronous On-Demand modules. All of its curriculum and professional development is provided at no cost. NGPF has been recognized by Common Sense Education as a &quot;Top Website for Teachers to Find Lesson Plans&quot; and also named NGPF a &quot;Selection for Learning.&quot; Visit ngpf.org for more.

Mission 2030
SENTENCE
NGPF’s mission is that by the year 2030, ALL students will take a one semester personal finance course before graduating from high school.
PARAGRAPH
NGPF’s mission is that by the year 2030, ALL students will take a one semester personal finance course before graduating from high school. The NGPF community is working to reach this goal through a combination of grassroots efforts - teachers championing the importance of teaching financial capability for all - and statewide legislative efforts to change course guarantees led by the Mission 2030 Fund, a nonprofit that advocates for this policy in all states.

State of Financial Education Report
SENTENCE
Each year, NGPF and Montana State University researchers analyze 12,000+ course catalogs from U.S. public high schools to determine exactly how many students are actually receiving a full semester of personal finance education – the gold standard of Mission 2030.
BULLETS
NGPF’s State of Financial Education Report creates an annual snapshot of progress toward Mission 2030
Done in collaboration with Montana State University to analyze 12,000+ course catalogs from U.S. public high schools
Shows the progress and glaring gaps in financial education access
PARAGRAPH
NGPF’s State of Financial Education Report creates an annual snapshot of the progress toward Mission 2030 – that by 2030, ALL students will take a one semester personal finance course before graduating high school. NGPF collaborates with Montana State University to collect and analyze 12,000+ course catalogs from U.S. public high schools to examine both the progress and the glaring gaps in financial education access.
